Questions

Do you take walk-ins?
Yes, during opening hours (Monday to Friday 9:00 AM to 8:30 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM, closed Sunday), subject to availability.
How do I guarantee a time?
Book online or call 902-330-6261.
Which services suit a walk-in?
Manicures and nail polish are quick. Longer services such as biogel sets are best booked ahead so a technician is free for the full time.
Do the offers take walk-ins?
No. The Senior Pamper Combo and Thursday Happy Hour need advance booking; see their offer pages for the notice required.
