Thursday Happy Hour: 20% off every Thursday

Gracy Nails & Beauty Salon in Charlottetown takes 20% off every service except the Senior Pamper Combo on Thursdays from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, November 1, 2026 to April 30, 2027. Book at least 12 hours ahead. The discount is applied at the counter.

  • 20% off every service on the price list, except the Senior Pamper Combo.
  • Thursdays, 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
  • Valid November 1, 2026 to April 30, 2027.
  • Book at least 12 hours in advance.
  • Thursday Happy Hour does not apply in December or on holidays.

Thursday Happy Hour questions

Does Gracy Nails & Beauty Salon have a Thursday discount?
Yes. Thursday Happy Hour gives 20% off every service on Thursdays from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
Which services are included in Thursday Happy Hour?
All services on our price list except the Senior Pamper Combo, which is a separate offer and does not combine with Thursday Happy Hour.
Do I need to book ahead for Thursday Happy Hour?
Yes. Book at least 12 hours before your Thursday appointment online or by phone.
When does Thursday Happy Hour run?
From November 1, 2026 to April 30, 2027. It does not apply in December or on holidays.

Senior Pamper Combo: $65 for manicure and pedicure

The Senior Pamper Combo at Gracy Nails & Beauty Salon in Charlottetown is a regular manicure and a regular pedicure for $65 (regular $80, 90 minutes), Mondays and Tuesdays 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, November 1, 2026 to April 30, 2027. Book at least 24 hours ahead. Holidays are excluded.

  • 1 regular manicure ($35 value)
  • 1 regular pedicure ($45 value)
  • Package price $65, regular $80: save $15 (about 19% off).
  • Mondays and Tuesdays, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, November 1, 2026 to April 30, 2027.
  • Appointments only: there are no walk-ins for the Senior Pamper Combo, and it needs at least 24 hours advance booking.
  • It is not part of Thursday Happy Hour and does not combine with it.

Senior Pamper Combo questions

What is the Senior Pamper Combo?
A package of a regular manicure and a regular pedicure for $65 instead of $80, about 90 minutes, in quiet salon hours. "Senior" is the name of the package; there is no age limit.
When is the Senior Pamper Combo available?
Mondays and Tuesdays, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, November 1, 2026 to April 30, 2027.
Can I walk in for the Senior Pamper Combo?
Appointments only: there are no walk-ins for the Senior Pamper Combo, and it needs at least 24 hours advance booking.
Can I use Thursday Happy Hour on the Senior Pamper Combo?
It is not part of Thursday Happy Hour and does not combine with it.
