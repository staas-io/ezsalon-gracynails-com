Questions

How long does a pedicure take?
Each pedicure's time is listed in the price table above.
What is the difference between a regular and a spa pedicure?
A regular pedicure includes cuticle care, nail shaping, a pedi scrub and a massage. A spa pedicure adds an exfoliant, a sugar scrub, a mask and a hot stone massage.
Can I get a shellac pedicure?
Yes. Pedicure shellac and spa pedicure shellac are on the price list, with shellac instead of regular polish.
Do you take walk-ins for pedicures?
Yes, during opening hours (Monday to Friday 9:00 AM to 8:30 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM, closed Sunday), subject to availability. Book online or call 902-330-6261 to guarantee your time.
