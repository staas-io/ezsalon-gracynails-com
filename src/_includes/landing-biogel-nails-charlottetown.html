Questions

Do you do acrylic nails?
No. We do not offer acrylic sets. We offer biogel full sets, overlays and refills, Gel X, and dipping powder. We can remove acrylic done elsewhere; see Biogel / acrylic removal in our price list.
What is the difference between biogel and acrylic?
Acrylic is a liquid and powder mixed on the nail that hardens in the air. Biogel is a gel applied in thin layers and cured under a lamp. We chose biogel because we consider it gentler on natural nails.
What are the different kinds of manicure?
Basic (no polish), regular (regular polish) and shellac (gel polish cured under a lamp) manicures, spa manicures with extra care, biogel, and dipping powder. Every price and time is listed above.
Is shellac included with biogel?
Yes. Biogel full set, overlay and refill prices include Shellac.
Do you take walk-ins?
Yes, during opening hours (Monday to Friday 9:00 AM to 8:30 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM, closed Sunday), subject to availability. Book online or call 902-330-6261 to guarantee your time.
